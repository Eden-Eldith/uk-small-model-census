\begin{abstract}
Company-based inventories cannot by themselves describe all domestic AI
research capability. We examine a repository-first route to discovering
model builders, including individuals without registered companies. On
30 October 2026 we enumerated every Hugging Face model returned by the
English, text-generation, PyTorch, under-one-billion-parameter and
``Base only'' filters. Complete API and website listings agreed on
917 repositories from 495 publishing accounts. Public-source research
covered every account and identified eight individual publishers with
explicit UK locations. Model-specific follow-through found 16 repositories
linked to UK-located people across 11 publisher accounts, including
co-authors and one dataset/applications contributor; 13 further repositories
had UK affiliation evidence and one had conflicting location evidence.
We retain 245 unresolved account locations. The findings include
documented laptop-CPU pretraining, independent oscillating-activation
research on consumer GPUs, task fine-tunes and institutional collaborations.
Catalogue tags alone did not establish training origin or even natural-language
scope. We release the complete cohort, source-linked geographic evidence,
37 model assessments and reproducible code. The study supplies a concrete
way to include released individual work in a broader map of UK AI capability,
while keeping model provenance, contributor roles and unresolved cases visible.
\end{abstract}
