\section{Finding the builders through their models}
I used the 30 October 2026 snapshot of \textbf{917 model repositories from
495 publishing accounts}. My saved query selected Text Generation,
English, PyTorch, the displayed under-1B parameter range, Base only, and
Recently Updated, with no name search. The ten API pages and 31 website
pages agreed on membership. This saved cohort is the population analysed
throughout the paper.

Hugging Face is the GitHub of open-source models: a public place where
builders publish weights, model cards and links to their training code.
It is an obvious starting point for discovering who already builds models.
I chose the PyTorch filter to make the search manageable and target
work comparable to my consumer-hardware development route.

In my 31 October exploration, adding Transformers under the final
combination reduced the visible result to 552 and hid WiggleGPT; removing
that additional library constraint restored it. Removing PyTorch in a
later configuration produced 7,022
results. An existing model disappeared when an additional library filter
was selected, then reappeared when it was removed. Discovery depends on
where and how the search is conducted. The practical task is to inspect
the released work and identify its builders.

I began classification with the downloaded CSV/JSONL records, model cards and
profiles. Clear organisations, derivatives and overseas connections can be
recorded immediately. Existing investigations are reused; targeted additional
sources are needed only for unanswered fields that could change a relevant
classification. Provenance distinguishes new-initialisation training from
fine-tuning, continued pretraining, distilled or reused weights, mirrors and
untrained checkpoints. A catalogue's Base tag is not proof of original
training. Saved checkpoint comparisons establish the GPT-2 and T5 derivative
cases discussed below.

I directed the location follow-through across all 151 previously unresolved
accounts. The agents began with each frozen model's card and community
discussion, then followed the creator's profile and linked GitHub, personal
or social pages. I set a maximum of three minutes for each of the final
117 accounts, with three agents working on separate accounts, and retained
the earlier 34 completed investigations.

Broader creator activity was consulted when the model-specific
sources and profile links left the question unanswered. Country and regional
estimates carry their basis and confidence. A final local-only reading of
the saved model cards resolved straightforward provenance gaps across the
rest of the frozen cohort. Original training is recorded alongside inherited
architecture, implementation and checkpoints, so a standard recipe trained
from fresh weights can be distinguished from modifying another builder's
weights.

I then directed a final targeted discovery pass for unanswered country and
origin fields, using exact creator-name and handle searches, relevant
linked profiles, model files and community replies. I kept the three-minute
account limit and reused completed investigations throughout this pass.

\emph{UK-built} concerns the builder's location while developing and running
the model. Overseas rented GPUs are recorded separately. Dated study or
employment overlapping development is distinguished from direct run-location
evidence and from a current profile with no historical link. \emph{University
ever} includes incomplete attendance anywhere. \emph{Institutional route}
includes university, laboratory, company or relevant industry affiliation,
including a technology day job when the model was a home project.
Affiliation at build time, project backing, funding and compute source are
recorded individually.

The strict-independent group requires at least one genuinely from-scratch
natural-language model built in the UK, no university attendance, and no
institutional affiliation or backing, with independence affirmatively
supported by the sources. Counts use
models for provenance, accounts for the complete inventory, and deduplicated
people for researcher routes. Exclusion reasons can overlap; everyone remains
visible in the full 495-account companion appendix.
