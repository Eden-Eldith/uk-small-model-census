\section{A bounded, evidence-led discovery method}
The 30 October 2026 snapshot contains \textbf{917 model repositories from
495 publishing accounts}. The saved query selected Text Generation,
English, PyTorch, the displayed under-1B parameter range, Base only, and
Recently Updated, with no name search. The ten API pages and 31 website
pages agreed on membership. That membership remains frozen; later live
searches do not replace it.

The PyTorch filter is intentional. In the author's 31 October exploration,
adding Transformers under the final combination reduced the visible result
to 552 and hid WiggleGPT; removing that additional library constraint
restored it. Removing PyTorch in a later configuration produced 7,022
results. These are recorded live observations illustrating filter sensitivity,
not alternative census populations or estimates of all UK models.

Classification begins with the downloaded CSV/JSONL records, model cards and
profiles. Clear organisations, derivatives and overseas connections can be
recorded immediately. Existing investigations are reused; targeted additional
sources are needed only for unanswered fields that could change a relevant
classification. Provenance distinguishes new-initialisation training from
fine-tuning, continued pretraining, distilled or reused weights, mirrors and
untrained checkpoints. A catalogue's Base tag is not proof of original
training. Saved checkpoint comparisons inform the GPT-2 and T5 derivative
cases; no repeat weight downloads were needed for this revision.

\emph{UK-built} concerns the builder's location while developing and running
the model. Overseas rented GPUs are recorded separately. Dated study or
employment overlapping development is distinguished from direct run-location
evidence and from a current profile with no historical link. \emph{University
ever} includes incomplete attendance anywhere. \emph{Institutional route}
includes university, laboratory, company or relevant industry affiliation,
including a technology day job when the model was a home project.
Employment does not itself demonstrate that the employer paid for training.
Affiliation at build time, project backing, funding and compute source remain
separate fields.

The strict-independent group requires at least one genuinely from-scratch
natural-language model built in the UK, no university attendance, and no
institutional affiliation or backing, with independence affirmatively
supported. A missing biography is not proof of independence. Counts use
models for provenance, accounts for the complete inventory, and deduplicated
people for researcher routes. Exclusion reasons can overlap; everyone remains
visible in the full 495-account companion appendix.
